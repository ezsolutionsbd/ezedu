Questions schools ask

Do guardians need a smartphone?

A smartphone gives the full guardian app. SMS alerts for basic phones are coming soon.

Do we need to install anything?

No. EzEdu opens in the browser, and you can add it to the home screen like an app.

Can we use it on a computer and a phone at the same time?

Yes. Open EzEdu in any browser on a computer, and keep using it on your phone. Both show the same data, so the office can work on a laptop while teachers use their phones.

Can we bring our existing student list?

Yes. Import students from a spreadsheet (CSV), and EzEdu creates their IDs.

Does it work without internet?

Attendance works offline and syncs when the connection returns. Other screens need a connection.

Can we run more than one branch?

Yes. School groups can manage branches together and share staff between them.
